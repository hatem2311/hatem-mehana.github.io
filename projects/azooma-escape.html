Project Overview

  • First Saudi stealth comedy game developed by ASH Studio.
  • Transforms a traditional family gathering (Azooma) into a stealth escape adventure.
  • Unique twist: Relatives replace guards – armed with food, questions, and endless hospitality.
  • Core gameplay: hide under tables, read NPC patterns, dodge crowds, and use teacups as last-resort tools.
  • Environments: vibrant courtyards, festival streets, and lively living rooms inspired by Saudi culture.
  • Tone: lighthearted, funny, and culturally authentic while delivering classic stealth tension.
  • Vision: showcase Saudi identity through comedy and chaos, creating a globally accessible gaming experience.
