Project Overview

  • A strategy game that simulates ancient battles using squares as units
  • Five categories of units with different offensive and defensive abilities.
  • Ten historical armies, each with a unique ability.
  • Thirty levels with increasing difficulty.
  • Offline battles focused on pre-battle planning.
  • Option to hold and deploy units during the fight.
  • Available now on Google Play.
